Socratica

An AI learning engine on spaced repetition and my own Socratic method. I am its first user.

Material
Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy (async), PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker Compose
Status
In build, cycle 4 of 15

An AI learning engine built on spaced repetition and my own Socratic teaching method: the agent asks before it answers, so the understanding stays with the learner. I am its first user, which keeps the product honest.

FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis and Docker Compose on a 15-cycle roadmap toward retrieval and agent orchestration. Three cycles shipped: a three-service dev environment with named-volume persistence, hot reload and health checks, then a layered FastAPI skeleton with an async engine and pooled connections. REST design and authentication are in progress. Every milestone is proven with logs and curl, never assumed.

This sheet

Brushed titanium in WebGL2, lit by your cursor. Spring physics, kinetic type, a console.

Material
WebGL2, GLSL, CSS, vanilla JavaScript; AI-assisted
Status
Rev F, 2026

A dependency-free, single-file portfolio drawn as an engineering sheet. A WebGL2 shader renders brushed titanium with anisotropic highlights and heat-tint colour, and your cursor is the only light source: the name breathes toward it on a variable-font width axis, plates cast shadows away from it, buttons lean into it.

Concept, spec and acceptance are mine; the code was generated with Claude Code and verified with jsdom smoke tests and the Khronos GLSL validator. Press / anywhere to open the console.

4

Nifty Composer

A music composition tool rebuilt for a real client with a six-person Scrum team at UNSW. Graded Distinction.

Material
React, TypeScript, VexFlow, Tone.js; FastAPI and music21 for MusicXML
Status
Delivered, 2026

A web-based music composition tool rebuilt from scratch for a real client, a music educator, with a six-person Scrum team in the UNSW capstone. Score rendering with VexFlow, playback with Tone.js, sprints tracked in Jira, delivered through a branch-and-pull-request workflow. Graded Distinction.

My part: MIDI export on the frontend with @tonejs/midi, a MusicXML import service in FastAPI with music21, and save and load of local JSON project files. Re-reading what the client had actually asked for showed that nobody had requested login or authentication, so the team moved from a full backend to a hybrid: frontend for most exports, FastAPI only where MusicXML parsing needed it.
